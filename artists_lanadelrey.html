Lana Del Rey

/ BIOGRAPHY /

Lana Del Rey, or by her biological name Elizabeth Woolridge Grant, is an American singer-songwriter well known for her cinematic musical style, poetic lyrics and nostalgic additions to pop music.

Born on June 21st, 1985 in New York, she developed an interest in music as she grew up, leading her to take part in club performances and write songs whilst studying at Fordham University. Before she adopted her professional name, she performed under aliases like Lizzy Grant.

In 2011, she released Video Games, causing widespread attention due to its dreamy production and melancholic atmosphere.

In 2012, her debut album, Born to Die, was released, recognized by a mix of orchestral arrangements, alternative pop, and nostalgic imagery.

Throughout her career, she has been continuously experimenting with different sounds and composing lyrics. Albums that demonstrate her evolving approach include Ultraviolence, Honeymoon and Did you Know That There's a Tunnel Under Ocean Blvd.
