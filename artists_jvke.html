JVKE

/ BIOGRAPHY /

JVKE, or Jacob Dodge Lawson, is an uprising artist, songwriter and a social media personality. He has been well known for his expressive vocals and approach on classical instruments.

Born on March 3, 2001, in Providence, Rhode Island, JVKE grew up in a family with a history of music. His mother was a music teacher, while his father was a pastor. He began learning instruments at a young age, developing an interest in piano, drums and guitar. His early musical experiences, including performing with his family and participating in church music, helped shape his passion for songwriting and production.

JVKE initially gained attention through social media during the 2020 pandemic lockdowns. Working alongside his mother, he created musical mash-ups, remixes and original compositions that gradually attracted a large online audience. His original song Upside Down became particularly popular, helping introduce his music to listeners beyond social media.

In 2022, he released golden hour, a piano-led pop ballad featuring soaring vocals, layered instrumentation and orchestral elements. The song became his breakthrough hit, reaching the Billboard Hot 100's top ten. Its success established JVKE as a rising figure in contemporary pop music.
