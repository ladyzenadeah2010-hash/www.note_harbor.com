Cafune

/ BIOGRAPHY /

Cafune consists of an indie pop duo, singer-songwriter Sedona Schat and producer Noah Yoo, originally meeting in New York University. They are well recognized for their single hit, Tek It, in 2022, having gone viral across the public.

“Cafuné” is a Brazilian-Portuguese word that translates to “the act of running your fingers through a loved one’s hair.” When Sedona and Noah Yoo came upon the word while looking for a name for their college project in Brooklyn, they never anticipated that their 2015 EP "Love Songs for Other People" would connect with a Brazilian fanbase a continent away.

Noah Yoo: He was raised in Virgina, playing violin and performing guitar in high school bands.

Sedona Schat: She grew up in Nevada, finding a love for music when she was young. She was recognized in her hometown for her local performances before she left to New York for college.

Throughout their debut album "Running", Cafuné embrace their blend of alternative pop, soft rock, and shoegaze, anchored by Sedona’s dreamy vocals.
